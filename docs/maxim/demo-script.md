# Recruiter Demonstration Proposal

Target: November 19, 2026. Proposed duration: 10-12 minutes; owner approval and
two final rehearsals remain outstanding. Do not call this an approved script.

## Scope

Show full-stack problem solving: a real Astryx/Next dashboard and conversational
agent, authenticated PostgreSQL commands/worker, Rust policy validation, retained
Career-Ops evaluation/PDFs, remembered approved answers and native agent-browser
form preparation. Use a clearly labeled **isolated synthetic employer form**
until a permitted live form variant is certified. No real credentials, private
mail/contact data, employer-proprietary evidence or access keys on screen.

## Sequence

1. State the problem: repeated application data and forgotten follow-ups. Show
   Candidate source/fact provenance and an explicitly approved professional fact.
2. Search a declared company feed or open a saved role. Show freshness/coverage
   limitations rather than claiming every internet job is searchable.
3. Ask the Agent to read the role and evaluate its UUID. Show that Career-Ops
   supplies the score, while Rust validates policy and maps its tier.
4. Review the report, evidence and actual tailored PDF. Approve exact hashes in
   the UI. Explain that the model cannot perform this human approval itself.
5. Authorize the dedicated visible browser. Ask the Agent to fill approved fields
   in that session. Show the real form and exact selected PDF, not a video/mock.
6. Reach an unknown question. Save/review/approve it in Answers, confirm scoped
   reuse and fill it. Correct/revoke the answer and demonstrate stale-use denial.
7. Pause/resume, then take manual control. Leave the synthetic application at
   review. Show the tracker still says not submitted; never imply filling applied.
8. Show optional features only within their verified scope: aggregate Discord
   preview, selected-thread Gmail setup/evidence, manual networking drafts,
   small-sample-aware analytics and private interview practice.
9. Close with the architecture and tests/recovery. Show one explicit failure
   (unsupported form or unconfigured model) that retains work and explains the
   next action without sample fallback.

## Go/No-Go

Use [verification](../../roadmap/reviews/mvp-completion-verification.md) as the
checklist. Docker, final integration/UI runs, two rehearsals and screen-share
review must be green. Only show live Gmail/Discord delivery after explicit account
and destination consent plus actual proof. A fixture is a fixture; no broad ATS,
autonomous submission or live-model-quality claim follows from it.
